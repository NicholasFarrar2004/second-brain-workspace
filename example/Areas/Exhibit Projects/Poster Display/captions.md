## Poster 1: Observe

Freash observations start with careful attention. Describe what you can see before deciding what it means.

## Poster 2: Measure

Use the same unit when comparing two measurements. Write down the unit alongside each value.

## Poster 3: Compare

Look for similarities and differences. A useful comparison says what stayed the same and what changed.

## Poster 4: Explain

Connect your explanation to the observations. If the evidence is incomplete, say what you would check next.
